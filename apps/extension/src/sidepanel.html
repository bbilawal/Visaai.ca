VisaAI Portal Assistant

Open the portal in your current tab and sign in, then paste the session token from your VisaAI dashboard. You always complete payment & submission.

On the IRCC portal

  • IRCC signs you out after about 20 minutes without activity, and anything not saved on that page is lost. Save each IRCC page before you switch to VisaAI or another tab.
  • If your session expired, sign in again and open your application from your IRCC account with Continue on the draft. Bookmarked or deep links to a form page go to an error page.
  • Launch once and keep this panel open: each IRCC page fills as you reach it. You press Save and continue yourself — unless you turn on Auto-advance, which presses it for you after a page fills cleanly and stops at the Summary. It never submits, signs or pays.

On the eTA form

  • The eTA form is one page and needs no IRCC account. It starts over if left idle too long, so keep this tab and the panel open until you finish.
  • New questions appear as you answer. Press the form's Next yourself, then Fill newly shown questions here.
  • The declaration, your signature and the payment are yours — the assistant never does them.
